Gobble Dash — a turkey's great escape: Tom the turkey outruns the farmer from the farmyard, through the barn, out of the kitchen and into the woods, 1800 m to freedom. Jump fences and ovens, time the swinging carving knife, hide in haystacks while the farmer searches, and grab corn on the way — with a chasing farmer AI, three stages that get harder, synthesized WebAudio sound, and a title screen, live HUD and caught/escaped end screens with restart.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
